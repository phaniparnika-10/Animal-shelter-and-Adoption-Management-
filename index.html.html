About PawCare

A safe place. A second chance.

PawCare is an animal shelter and adoption management system designed to connect rescued animals with caring adopters. It brings animal records, adopter information, and adoption requests together in one organized place.

Our mission is to support animal welfare by helping shelters keep reliable records, share animals available for adoption, and review applications responsibly. Our vision is a community where every rescued animal has the chance to find a safe and loving home.

Care guide

A happy pet starts with good care

A few essentials to help adopters provide a safe, healthy, and supportive home.

🥣

Nutrition

Provide clean water and balanced food suitable for the animal's age and needs.

💉

Vaccination

Follow a veterinarian's vaccination and preventive-care schedule.

🧼

Grooming

Maintain coat, nails, ears, and dental hygiene with suitable care.

🩺

Vet checkups

Arrange regular veterinary visits and seek help when behavior or health changes.

🏡

Responsibility

Offer a safe environment, time, patience, exercise, and lifelong commitment.
